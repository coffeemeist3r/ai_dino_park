**Pick: BACKLOG-600 — the keeper in the ring** (Milestone 29 structure arc 2; the milestone's last spine arc).

The festival counts dinos only. The keeper can stand at the pond through the whole two hours and the park does not
notice: no line, no memory, no friendship, and the tales afterwards are identical whether the keeper came or not.
That makes the festival the one collective event the player cannot take part in.

**Scope (arc-sized, playable):**
- The keeper counts as an attendee the first time, while the festival is open, that it stands in the bowl within the
  festival's outer ring (Chebyshev ≤ `SULK_RING` of the festival tile) — not only at the opening instant, so a player
  who walks over after the banner still makes it.
- On arrival: the leader turns and greets the keeper in its register (bubble + ticker), every attendee gains a small
  keeper-friendship tick for the shared day, and each files *"the keeper came"*.
- At the close each attendee's festival tale carries whether the keeper was there; the next greet says *"you were
  there"* — or, for a keeper who missed it, *"you weren't there"*, in the dino's register.
- No save change: memories already persist; the flag is per-festival and transient.

**Ten-minute answer (fresh save):** the spring festival opens two real minutes in. Walk to the pond: Sunny turns,
*"The keeper came too!"*, and hearts tick on every dino in the ring. Don't, and every greet after noon ends *"You
weren't there."*

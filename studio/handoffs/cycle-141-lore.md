*where does it happen?* — and the lore track's share of that is the smallest, most-pressed version: the
park's dinos are each running a readable activity every tick, the keeper walks up and presses E, and the
hello that comes back is the same hello it would have been if the dino had been asleep, gathering, eating,

screen as a glyph, and the dialogue does not know it. That is a distinctness gap, not a UI gap: what a dino

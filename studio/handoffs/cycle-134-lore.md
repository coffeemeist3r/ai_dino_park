# Cycle 134 — Lore Handoff

**Theme:** The last lore arc of Milestone 14, and the one that hands the whole thing to the player. Two
cycles have made the tic a real object in this park: 412 gave it a *cause* (a dino that came away empty from
a contested drop falls into its ritual sooner), and 407 gave it *legs* (watch a close friend at its ritual
often enough and you pick up a faint echo of it — the first behaviour in this park's history to travel
sideways between two living dinos). What neither did was give it a **name**. The tic is still, forty-seven
cycles on, a thing you have to be standing in the right place at the right minute to have ever seen. Nothing
in the park records that Thornback paces a fixed little path and Mossback turns three times before settling;
close the tab and that distinctness is gone.

BACKLOG-409 puts it in the book. The collection book (021) is where this park stores what the player has
*collected* about a dino, and a signature tic is the most collectible fact any dino now owns — it is
name-seeded, so it is stable; it is per-dino, so it separates them; and after 407 it can be **borrowed**,
which means the book line is not merely a label but a small piece of social history: *"turns three times
before settling — the same as Thornback."* That is the line the milestone has been building toward. A tic
the player never saw is a tic that never happened, and the book is the only surface in this game whose job
is to make sure that isn't true.

The discipline for the Designer: the book names what the player has **witnessed**, not what the save
happens to contain. A dino's tic entry should be earned the way the rest of the book is earned — you have to
have been there — or the collection stops meaning collection and becomes a save-file dump.

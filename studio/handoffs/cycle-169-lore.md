# Cycle 169 — Lore Handoff

**Theme: a cry that can be found, and a cry that is answered.**

The park has had a distress call since cycle 44 and it has always been a thing that
happens *to the dinos*. A dino shivers or startles, it cries out, its closest friend
turns and walks toward it — and unless the keeper happened to be looking at that patch
of ground, none of it reaches the player at all. The bowl's one genuinely urgent noise
is also its most private one.

This cycle closes both halves of Milestone 22's third arc. **BACKLOG-204** gives the cry
a line in the ticker naming who called, so a keeper standing in the wrong zone can find
the dino in trouble instead of discovering it later as a mood. **BACKLOG-202** gives the
friend that turns a voice: before it takes a step it calls back, in its own call, so the
bond is audible in both directions rather than inferable from a walk. The two are one
beat — the cry goes out, something answers it, and you hear both.

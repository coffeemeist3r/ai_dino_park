# Cycle 138 — Lore Handoff

**Theme:** The ritual stops being a pin. For fifty cycles the tic has had a cause (412),
a way to travel (407), a witness (416), a name in the book (409), and — last cycle — a
price for being found (422). Every one of those beats happens *at the same tile*. A dino
that has spent six separate stretches alone in the Grove turns the same slow circle on the
same square of grass every time, and the ground never learns anything about it. This cycle
the anchor **wanders**: the little path migrates across the ground over successive
stretches, so the ritual reads as a habit a living animal keeps rather than a loop a
program runs. It is also the beat 496's worn-ground art is waiting on — a mark under a
fixed anchor is a smudge; a mark under a drifting one is a path.

**Reachability note for the Designer (CHARTER v7):** a drift phrased as "slowly, over many
stretches" is the exact shape the reachability bar rejects. Whatever the drift rule is, the
**second** solitary stretch must anchor somewhere visibly different from the first, and the
cumulative wander must be legible inside one ten-minute watch — not a per-stretch epsilon
that needs an hour to add up to a tile.

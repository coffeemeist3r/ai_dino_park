# Cycle 139 — Lore Handoff

**Theme:** The ritual has learned to be interrupted. 408 gave being *caught by the keeper*
a bashful frame, 420 gave it a register that climbs across a stretch, 422 gave it a price —
and every one of those is about the keeper. The other way a solitary stretch ends is the
one the park has had since 405 and has never once made a moment of: another dino wanders
into range, the solitude breaks, and `resetTic` silently drops the whole stretch on the
floor. Nothing is filed, nothing floats, the ticker says nothing, and the dino that was
turning a slow circle by itself thirty seconds ago greets you as though the last minute
never happened. This cycle the *ending* of a stretch becomes as legible as its start:
a dino pulled out of its ritual by a friend walking up is **glad of the company**, and it
leads its next greeting with it.

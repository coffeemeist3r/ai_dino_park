catching one cycle late.

beat's trigger is a player action (`K`, re-pick a different observer) rather than a clock. That is
reachable in ten minutes — the picker is one key away from boot — but the Designer must make sure
the *consequence* is too, and not gated behind a friendship level a new save cannot have reached.
A switch on day 1, with nobody fond of anybody yet, must still produce something the park says.

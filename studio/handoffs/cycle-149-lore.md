**Suggested next-up:** **BACKLOG-121 — keeper-shaped routine.** A dino learns the real hour you usually come
back and drifts to the front of the glass around then, anticipating you.

Why this one, why now, and the one reshaping it needs. 121 is the arc's named partner and it is the only
queued item where *who is awake* changes **who does the thing**, rather than changing the sky the thing
happens under. The vigil is not owl-exclusive and must not be — that was 524's lesson, and making the beat a
property of a trait rather than of an hour is the mistake this arc keeps being offered. It is
chronotype-shaped by construction instead: **a dino that is down cannot keep a vigil.** Come back at eight in
the morning and it is one of the Bowl's four day-dinos at the hatch, because Rex is asleep. Come back while
the park is dark and the only cast that *can* be standing there is an owl. Same rule, opposite halves of the
roster, and no branch in the code that mentions either word.

**The reshaping, and it is a CHARTER v7 one.** The item says *a very-high-friendship dino*. Shipped
literally, that is an invisible feature: a fresh save's friendship book is empty, so the item's own gate is a
gate nothing passes on frame one, and the park would carry a system that switches on some hours after the
bar stops watching. So friendship **grades** the vigil rather than gating it — the fondest waking dino keeps
it, and with nobody yet fond, the hatch's own ground keeps it by name order. The keeper's hour is the same
story: two visits are needed to have an hour at all, so the **founding save records the boot itself as a
prior visit**, derived from the hour the park opens on rather than from a number anybody typed. Both halves
of the item survive; neither ships dark.

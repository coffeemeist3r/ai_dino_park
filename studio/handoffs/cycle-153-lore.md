its own account of it, 113 gave the digest a cold half, and 152's art gave the withheld thought a rig that
says *I am not telling you* instead of a faded version of *I missed you*. All of it arrives in one dialog

best writing away.

**Suggested next-up (lore track):** **BACKLOG-114 — the away-log in the book.**

Why this one and not 119 or 122:

- **It is the arc that has just become worth reading.** At cycle 149 the digest was one line about time
  passing. Since then it has grown a warm half that reaches a five-minute absence, a cold half that names
  a pair who drifted, a per-dino account with three registers, and a spoilage/upkeep tail. Every one of
  those is written to a modal that is dismissed by the next keypress. The book already keeps everything
  else this park knows about a dino permanently; the one thing it does not keep is the only thing the park
  ever says about *you*.
- **119 wants a bookend the player cannot watch.** The goodbye glance fires on `visibilitychange → hidden`.
  A mark drawn on the frame the tab stops being drawn is the reachability bar's hardest case, and it
  deserves a cycle where somebody has thought about what "the player sees it" even means for a hidden tab.
  Not this one.
- **122 wants a real-world calendar day boundary.** BACKLOG-529 shipped the keeper's own local hour at cycle
  152, so 122 is genuinely unblocked now — but "returning on consecutive real days" cannot be watched inside
  a ten-minute window without a hook that lies about the date. It is the right next item and the wrong item
  to rush; it wants a cycle that designs its own dev seam.

114's own bar answer is inside the window and does not need a fixture to fake: step away for five minutes,
come back, dismiss the digest **without reading it**, press V, and the book's first block is what you just
threw away. That is the item's literal subject — *re-read what the bowl got up to without having caught the
digest live* — and the fresh save reaches it in five minutes.

**A note for the Designer:** the item says "the last digest". Keep the *last few*. The park now returns
different news depending on how long the gap was, and one entry that overwrites the previous one makes the
book a mirror of the modal rather than a record. Three is enough to see that the news changed.

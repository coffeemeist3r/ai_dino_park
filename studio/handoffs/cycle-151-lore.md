cycle — every dino now keeps its own account of the keeper being gone, and wears a thought
about it. The arc that follows is the one that keeps the milestone honest: an absence that
only ever produces warmth is a compliment, not a consequence. Two dinos who were already
barely a pair, left alone together for a week of park-time, should come back *further apart*,

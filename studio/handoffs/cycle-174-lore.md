**Theme: friendship has an opposite.**

Milestone 23 gave the park a shape for liking: the book names who is close to whom, and the graph moves while you
watch. But the park has only ever had one direction. Every pairwise number in it measures warmth; the nearest thing
to a dislike is a "wary" read buried in the hatch's memory ring, which no page shows and no dino acts on outside a
scramble. Real company has a few pairs who simply do not get on, and that is half of what makes the warm pairs
mean anything. Milestone 24 makes the cold pairs visible: who does not get on with whom, on the book page, and two
of them bristling at each other in the grass while the player watches.

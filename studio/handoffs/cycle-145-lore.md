# Cycle 145 — Lore Handoff

**Theme:** A place you were born and a place you walked to are not the same standing, and since last
night the park has been telling the player they are. Cycle 144 gave five grounds a recorded founder in
one stroke — and rendered every one of them through a sentence written for an *arrival*. Bramble's
block says "first across into the Grove" and Bramble has never crossed anything. This cycle the book
learns to tell the two apart, which is the smallest possible change to the text and the largest
available change to what the record *means*: the park would then distinguish, in the player's own
book, between somewhere you have always been and somewhere you chose.

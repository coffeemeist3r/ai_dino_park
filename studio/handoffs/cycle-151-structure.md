**What makes it unsplittable at a playable seam.** Every other structural item in this queue has a
half you can ship: draw one mark, wire one hook, place one prop. This one's subject is *an assumption
distributed across ~550 specs*, and a fixture seam that half the callers use is worse than no seam at
all — it is a third population, which is the precise failure the item was opened to stop. The item has
been paid for in roughly twelve scattered instalments (`gatherToBowl`, `emptyGrounds`,
`__seedGranaryReady`, and nine more by cycle 146's count) and every instalment made the next one
cheaper to defer and the whole more expensive to finish; two of them were about *time* rather than
founding state, a use the item never anticipated, which is what a fixture with no name does. The
splittable version of this item is the version that has already been shipped five times and did not
work. What it needs is a fire where nothing else is in flight, so the seam lands complete and every
existing caller moves onto it in the same commit.

**The reachability bar, answered — not waived.**

> *In a fresh save, watched for ten minutes, what does the player see that they could not see before?*

**Three grounds carry a visible banked heap on the first frame instead of one, and one of them is a
full three-step pile — the largest of the drawn steps, which no fresh park has ever shown.**

That is not a consolation prize bolted onto an infra item; it is the item's own proof. `FOUNDING_PILES`
currently stocks exactly one ground (`{ grove: { stone: 2 } }`), so of the three drawn `pile_1..3` rigs
the founding park exercises one, and `pile_3` — four units or more — has never existed on a fresh save
in the park's history. That is CHARTER v7's corollary verbatim: three steps drawn, the founding state
tuned to sit under two of them. It is also, precisely, a **founding constant**, and moving it is the
exact operation that reddened 16 unrelated specs in cycle 136 and opened this item. So the cycle builds
the seam and then moves the constant *through* it — the fallout the seam exists to absorb, absorbed on
the day the seam ships, or the seam does not ship.

The frontier ground (`saltpan`) stays bare: BACKLOG-505's unsettled read is a claim about residents and
founders, not piles, and nothing here may make the frontier look lived on.

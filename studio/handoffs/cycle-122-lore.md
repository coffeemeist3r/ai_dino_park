**Theme:** *Far is finally far.* Milestone 10 added a fourth ground and proved the systems generalized;
what it also exposed — in its own closing structure item (475) — is that every cross-zone read in the park
is **one hop deep**. A dino can only ever want the ground next door. Milestone 11 gives the chain

that can be too full to take one more mouth. The lore half is about what the walking *does* to a dino —
this cycle, the one that just came back and hasn't shaken the place off yet.
